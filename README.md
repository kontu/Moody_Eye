"Mad-eye moody" bi-directional eyeball prop


Ideas and references from
https://learn.adafruit.com/3d-printed-bionic-eye/overview
https://medium.com/@JeremySCook/diy-mad-eye-moody-eyeball-prop-from-harry-potter-5a9c9238e736


Built using
seeed_xiao atmelsam microboard
2x 5g micro servo's (smaller may work)


Servo's are glued together operating on 90' planes of each other. Used spring steel wire (piano wire) to connect from servo arms to eyeball.
Added jewlery screw eye screws to eyeball
Used a lego ball joint to hold eyeball center in place / provide pivot strength
30mm Doll Eye (hollow, half-sphere)
